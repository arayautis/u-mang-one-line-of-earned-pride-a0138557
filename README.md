## The line

> She had failed the piece a hundred times; tonight her hands found every note, the last chord rang through the hall, and her teacher — who never smiled — stood up first.

A student who has failed a piece a hundred times finally plays it through,
and the payoff is not applause but the strict teacher who never smiles
standing up first. The pride is earned inside the line — a hundred
failures, hands finding every note, a last chord ringing through the hall —
and it lands in one read, with the last words. One sentence, 168 characters
and 172 bytes as verified by running the program, no setup that overstays.
